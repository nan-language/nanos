# Roadmap

These tasks implement the specification of [docs/spec](docs/spec/00-charter.md), all but chapter 22, whose features NaN provides. Tasks 1 to 9 reach a system that boots in the sandbox and serves an agent on its serial link; tasks 10 to 17 complete the specification. Each task is one session, taken in order: it builds what its sentence names, to the rules of the chapters it cites, and ticks its box in the change that completes it. Completing a chapter means building what earlier tasks left of it.

1. [x] **Loader.** Build chapter 02, canonical rendering aside, as the `ident` package, and chapter 03's development loader, which boots `/kernel.elf` in the sandbox by reading and writing the boot record, verifying the `dev` candidate, drawing entropy and entering the kernel at its PVH entry with the handoff block, whose handoff record the kernel's `main` decodes through its validator.
2. [x] **Kernel core.** Implement chapter 04's kernel image, start sequence, CPUs, anchor, vectors, shared space, shootdowns, kernel policies, faults and halts, chapter 01's machine conditions, measured facts and derived defaults, chapter 05's frames, pools, mappings and core reserve, and chapter 08's run queues, CPU classes, wake placement, accounting, clocks, timers and idle, so the kernel starts the sandbox's four CPUs and runs its first task.
3. [x] **Runner.** Run components as instances in their own domains under chapter 06's runner, lifecycle, restarts and lazy start and chapter 04's integrity faults, with chapter 05's cells, limits, reserved instances, stacks and transfers, chapter 07's communication outside the isolated transport, chapter 09's principals, capability kinds, grants, revocation and Env construction, and chapter 17's core rings, and have `supervisor` start the base instances in chapter 20's start order from the genesis record the kernel image holds.
4. [x] **Devices.** Implement chapter 15's `devices` with discovery, hot plug, `devices.aml`, driver matching and instances, volumes, views and roles, interrupts and polling, unconfined DMA, device power and reset, the `Block`, `Console`, `Entropy` and `Rtc` protocols with their x86_64 drivers, and the `fat` component.
5. [x] **Store engine.** Implement the store of chapters 11 and 12 as a target-independent package tested on the host against an in-memory volume, all but the Store protocol, secret records, trial branches, placement, volume encryption and format upgrades.
6. [x] **Store and tree.** Run `store` with chapter 11's Store protocol on chapter 12's placement, commit chapter 13's genesis and serve all of its tree, `/live` included, with chapter 05's mapped blobs, keep chapter 06's checkpoints and dead letters in `/state`, and have `supervisor` reconcile `/sys/instances` and `/sys/config`.
7. [x] **Base services.** Add `journal` with chapter 17's events, event kinds, streams, causes, traces and metrics, `keys` with chapter 09's keys held in software, signatures, rotation and revocation and the store's secret records, `policy` evaluating chapter 19's policy and presets, and chapter 20's `power`, `clock` and a `boot` that records each boot in `/sys/boot` and `/live/boot`, so every base instance the start order runs before `link` converges at boot.
8. [x] **Link.** Serve chapter 18's link on the serial and port transports with chapter 20's shared `console`: framing, sessions under chapter 09's sessions as authority, requests, responses in chapter 02's canonical rendering, pushed records, queries and selectors, errors and fixes, budgets, wake rules, configuration and the verbs `hello`, `primer`, `describe`, `get`, `find`, `diff`, `history`, `why`, `watch` and `cancel`, the specification's transcripts of them passing as tests in the sandbox.
9. [ ] **Changes.** Implement chapter 14 with every plan step but `install`, `update`, `run` and `generation`, previewing through chapter 06's forks, behind the link's `plan`, `apply`, `call`, `undo`, `confirm` and `put`, so an owner session inspects, changes and reverts the running system over the link.
10. [ ] **Resources.** Complete chapters 08 and 05 with shares, reservations, deadlines, frequency, energy, memory pressure, hibernation memory and the memory summary, adding chapter 06's hibernation.
11. [ ] **Code.** Implement chapter 10 but the generation switch, with chapter 06's jobs, chapter 09's provenance and chapter 17's flight recorders and replay: `admission`, the `nan`, `scrub`, `selftest` and `replay` jobs of chapter 20, and the plan steps `install`, `update` and `run`.
12. [ ] **Isolation.** Implement chapter 04's isolated spaces, split tables, placement mechanics and user entry, chapter 07's isolated transport, chapter 09's placement, confidentiality of the shared space and costs of isolation, and chapter 15's DMA and IOMMU, `keys` running alone in its isolated space.
13. [ ] **Generations.** Complete chapter 03, the production loader, measurement, the health gate, recovery and `boot`'s generation and owner key operations included, with chapter 04's watchdog, chapter 10's generation switch and the plan step `generation`, chapter 11's trial branches, chapter 12's volume encryption and format upgrades, and chapter 15's `Tpm` protocol and `tpm-crb` driver, `keys` sealing its keys in the TPM.
14. [ ] **Oversight.** Complete chapter 19 with the owner, claim, enrollment, policy proposals, approvals, console PINs, taint, the rule of two, extraction, agent budgets and emergency controls, together with chapter 09's delegation, chapter 17's audit chain, chapter 18's `hello --claim`, `delegate`, `extract`, `approve` and `freeze`, and chapter 20's dedicated `console` and `infer`.
15. [ ] **Network.** Implement chapter 16 as `net`, with chapter 15's `Nic` protocol and its `virtio-net` and `virtio-vsock` drivers, chapter 18's vsock and TCP transports, and `clock`'s network time sources.
16. [ ] **AArch64.** Port the loader and the kernel to AArch64 through target variants, as the specification defines it for that architecture: the device-tree handoff and discovery, GICv3 and ITS, the generic timer, PSCI, SMMUv3, the SP805 watchdog and the `pluart` and `plrtc` drivers.
17. [ ] **Performance and conformance.** Implement chapter 21's `bench` job and the pipeline gating every change on it, meet every requirement and mechanism cost it states, and make each transcript of the specification and each classical equivalent of chapter 20 pass as a conformance test on the machines providing what it shows, as chapter 00 defines conformance.
