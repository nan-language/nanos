# 0116. Jobs

Status: superseded by 0121

## Context

Chapter 06 runs a program bundle's `main` as a job: an instance without a specification in `/sys/instances`, acting for the principal of the plan that ran it, its writes going to a branch merged when `main` returns. NaN loads a bundle through the kernel's `sys::load::Loader`, which is `Send` but not `Copy`, and runs a job entry with `nanos::run`, which only a kernel image can call. The runner, a library tested on the host, already creates instances, builds their worlds from their grants and wiring, redirects their Views for replays, and launches each incarnation through the kernel's `Components`, a `Copy` value. Its tasks run on stacks of fixed bounds, and its driver keeps store calls on tasks of their own. NaN's `x86_64-bare` boot heap, 16 MiB whatever the RAM, holds the runner's own memory and tasks besides every bundle the kernel loads (nan-language/nan#254), while the tasks of an instance's domain draw through its cell. A `run` step writes its `JobSpec` as a construction literal; NaN decodes a literal leaving fields out only into an `open` struct, a missing field taking the `Default` of its type, which no byte string, list, map or `Option` implements (nan-language/nan#251).

## Decision

- **Jobs as instances.** A `Control` order `Job` creates the job as an instance of the component `nanos::job` running the job's bundle, with no endpoint: no port reaches it. `Control.job` splits the `JobSpec` in its caller's task into the instance's specification and a `JobOrder` holding the rest, so the runner never holds a `JobSpec`. The runner writes the specification to `/state/<moniker>/job`, starts the job at once, and runs it again only as its `restart` asks.
- **Runs in two phases.** A task of the driver prepares each run: it writes the job record and creates `job/<moniker>` at the head of the job's branch through a View owning the nodes its owning operations write, which alone may create job branches. The driver then redirects the job's Views of its branch to `job/<moniker>` and launches it; the run's task reads the image `/sys/code/<hash>/image` through the job's cell, checking its blob id against the one admission recorded. When `main` ends, another task has the store merge the branch, as one commit for every job branch, deletes it, and writes the result.
- **Loading in the kernel.** `Components.run_job` takes the image and the input. In the kernel, a core task keeps the loader: it prepares the image unverified, since admission took it as trusted, loads it, and keeps the bundle until the job ends; `run_job` reaches it through a capability and runs `main` with `nanos::run` in the job's domain.
- **Defaults by type.** `JobSpec` is open, and `Budget`, `Share`, `Placement`, `Limits` and `Restart` default to what a job takes; `branch` and `keep_h` are optional, `main` and 24 when absent.
- **Class.** The class of a `run` step is the reversibility class its entry's digest declares, which the admission record's `Program` holds.

## Consequences

- A job runs in the shared space only, as its placement allows, until isolated spaces exist.
- Each job loads its own copy of its image; a second job of one bundle loads it again.
- A job's moniker is free again once the job has ended for good.
- A `run` literal spells the fields whose types have no `Default`: its bundle, input, arguments, environment, standard input, wiring, grants, branch, deadline and `keep_h`.
